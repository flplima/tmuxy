
   tmuxy — the missing tmux GUI

   You are watching a real tmux session, live, in a browser.
   This view is READ-ONLY: your keystrokes go nowhere.

   Things worth trying here:
     • drag the divider between panes
     • scroll this pane with the wheel, then select text with the mouse
     • open the tab overview
     • right-click a pane for its menu

   It runs in a throwaway container with nothing in it.
