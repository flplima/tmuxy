> [!WARNING]
> _tmuxy is under active development. Not ready for production. Read [[Security]] before you put it on a network._

**tmuxy** is a GUI for tmux, in a browser tab or a desktop app. Behind the scenes it's still tmux.

This wiki follows `main`, so a few things here are newer than the last release. Those are marked.
