# Invariants: Client Seams

The client-side rows of the invariant ledger (`C`, `Q`, `P`, `U`, `R`). Columns, prefixes and the rules that keep the ledger honest are in [INVARIANTS.md](INVARIANTS.md).

## C — client stream stage

| Id | Invariant | Kind | Stated in | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | A delta is applied only to the state it was computed against. A missing, misordered, repeated or undecodable delta is never applied; the stage refetches a full state and never paints a guess. | safety | `packages/tmuxy-ui/src/infra/transport/stateSequencer.ts`, `stateFeed.ts`, `src/domain/deltaProtocol.ts` | `stateSequencer.test.ts › asks for a resync on a sequence gap and applies nothing`, `› treats a payload that does not decode like a gap, and logs it`; `stateFeed.test.ts › a payload that does not decode is a gap: logged, never published, refetched`; `deltaProtocol.test.ts` (`isDeltaSeqGap`); `drivers/__tests__/HttpAdapter.test.ts › a state update that does not decode is a gap: the adapter refetches a full state` |
| C2 | A delta before any full state is ignored. A full state and an adopted answer are sync points numbered by the stream (`seq` on the state), so the delta right after one is checked like any other: a delta lost between a sync point and the next delta is a gap, never a delta applied over the hole. | safety | `stateSequencer.ts`, `deltaProtocol.ts` | `stateSequencer.test.ts › ignores a delta before any full state`, `› a delta lost right after a numbered sync point is a gap, not a delta applied over the hole`; `stateFeed.props.test.ts › never paints backwards…` (the property that found the hole: with an unnumbered sync point a delta lost right after it was applied over silently); `deltaProtocol.test.ts › no gap right after a full state (null prevSeq)` (the unnumbered case) |
| C3 | At most one refetch is in flight; deltas arriving while it is out are held (the newest, a bounded number) — neither published nor starting a second one — and a refetch that failed is asked for again on the next delta. | safety | `stateFeed.ts` (semaphore), `stateSequencer.ts` (`diverged`, `held`) | `stateFeed.props.test.ts › never paints backwards, never paints a guess, refetches at most once per loss, and ends on the server` (property over lost emissions, answer timing and failed answers: at most one refetch per loss and per failed answer, none for an answer the stream outran); `stateFeed.test.ts › a gap refetches one full state at the last viewport and publishes it`; `stateSequencer.test.ts › the deltas the stream delivers while a refetch is out apply on top of its numbered answer` |
| C4 | What is published after a refetch is the sequencer's state at that point — the answer, the held deltas above its number, and every delta queued behind it — so a paint never goes backwards and a stream that kept going during the refetch is caught up without another one; a delta lost among the held ones is a new gap, refetched once more. | ordering | `stateFeed.ts` (`RESYNCED` mark), `stateSequencer.ts` (`applyHeld`) | `stateFeed.props.test.ts › never paints backwards…` (property: the painted clock is monotone across every answer timing, and every paint is the server's whole state at that clock — each delta opens a window and closes an older one, so a delta skipped on the way leaves a trace) |
| C5 | Once the stream is synced, an initial-state answer never replaces stream state: it only fills a pane the stream has delivered no content for. Before the first full state, or after a gap, the answer is the state to start from. | ordering | `deltaProtocol.ts` (`adoptInitialState`), `stateSequencer.ts` (`adopt`) | `HttpAdapter.test.ts › an initial-state answer older than the stream does not blank a pane the stream filled`, `› …does not take back a window the stream added`, `› after a gap in the stream, the answer is the state to start again from`; `stateSequencer.test.ts › after a gap, applies no delta until an answer is adopted, then carries on from it`; `stateFeed.props.test.ts › never paints backwards…` (property: answers up to six emissions stale, landing before or after a full state, never paint backwards — the shape of both earlier fixes) |
| C6 | A new connection or a session switch starts a new sequence; an old delta seq never gates a new stream. | safety | `drivers/HttpAdapter.ts`, `drivers/TauriAdapter.ts` (`reopen`, `reset`) | — |
| C7 | The transport publishes every driver event in arrival order, and a subscription taken before `connect` misses nothing. On the web the one exception is that a state is held until the next frame, so a later non-state event may precede it; a state never precedes an earlier non-state event. | ordering | `TmuxTransport.ts`, `events.ts`, `stateFeed.ts` (`latestStatePerFrame`) | `TmuxTransport.test.ts › publishes what the driver pushes, in order, to a subscriber taken before it is pushed`; `stateFeed.test.ts › publishes a full state then deltas in sequence, other events in their place` (examples; the per-frame hold is —) |
| C8 | States are coalesced to one per display frame, the latest winning; an older state is never painted after a newer one. | ordering | `stateFeed.ts` (`latestStatePerFrame`) | — |
| C9 | A pane id appears at most once in any state the client holds, and a delta removes exactly the panes it nulls. | safety | `deltaProtocol.ts` (`applyDelta`) | `src/domain/__tests__/deltaProtocol.props.test.ts` (property) |
| C10 | A dropped connection is always retried with backoff; a pinged stream that goes silent is dropped and reopened; online, visibility and Retry reconnect at once; a stream the server refuses ends with its reason instead of retrying. There is never a rival `EventSource`. | liveness | `HttpAdapter.ts` | `HttpAdapter.test.ts › keeps retrying when a reconnect attempt fails to establish (no dead-loop)`, `› ten drops in a row each reconnect at once, on the real backoff schedule`, `› a pinged stream that goes silent is dropped and reopened`, `› a stream the server refuses ends in its reason…`, `› dedupes concurrent connect() calls into a single EventSource`; `tests/4-session-connectivity.test.js › six drops in a row → each reconnects within seconds…` [nightly] |

## Q — command queue and input

| Id | Invariant | Kind | Stated in | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Mutations leave the client strictly in issue order through one serial queue, so a split never lands in the tab a queued `select-window` is about to leave. | ordering | `HttpAdapter.ts`, `TauriAdapter.ts` (serial queue) | `HttpAdapter.test.ts › runs run_tmux_command through the serial queue in issue order` (example; Tauri is —); `tests/4-session-connectivity.test.js › 2 panes → reload → … → 3 rapid splits → UI synced` |
| Q2 | Keystroke batches ride the same queue as mutations, and a non-`send-keys` command first flushes every pending batch, so a keystroke never overtakes a queued mutation. | ordering | `TauriAdapter.ts`, `HttpAdapter.ts`, `infra/transport/keyBatching.ts` | `keyBatching.test.ts › flushAll drains pending batches and closes the windows` (the batcher only; the interleaving with a mutation is —) |
| Q3 | A lone keystroke is sent at once, never waiting out the batching window; order between literal and special-key batches is kept; hex-byte sends are never batched. | ordering | `keyBatching.ts` | `keyBatching.test.ts › sends an isolated literal keystroke immediately (leading edge)`, `› preserves literal→special cross-batch ordering`, `› preserves special→literal cross-batch ordering`, `› never intercepts hex-byte sends (-H)`; `tests/1-input-interaction.test.js › Type → backspace → Tab → Ctrl+C → Ctrl+D → arrow-up history` |
| Q4 | Reads (`query_tmux`) are not serialized on the client; their order against mutations holds because tmux answers them in-band on the one connection the mutations use (A6). | ordering | `HttpAdapter.ts` (`query`), `docs/DATA-FLOW.md` | — |

## P — client model

| Id | Invariant | Kind | Stated in | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | A placeholder (predicted) pane or window id is never sent to tmux as a target. | safety | `src/domain/commands.ts` (`toTmuxCommand`) | `machines/app/states/__tests__/dispatch.test.ts › leaves the target to tmux rather than naming a placeholder`, `› once tmux has confirmed them, names the client's own active window and pane` |
| P2 | An op is marked in flight before its adapter call and always settles: an unacked op is swept after `OP_STALE_TIMEOUT_MS`, an acked one after `OP_ACKED_STALE_TIMEOUT_MS`, and a rejected one rolls back. | liveness | `infra/store/TmuxStore.ts`, `domain/store/model.ts`, `domain/store/types.ts` | `domain/store/__tests__/model.test.ts › stale ops get rolled back after OP_STALE_TIMEOUT_MS`, `› in-flight ops survive the quick sweep but fall to the acked backstop`; `infra/store/__tests__/TmuxStore.test.ts › dispatch rolls back the op on TmuxError`, `› TransportError surfaces as OpTransportError and rolls back` |
| P3 | While ops are pending the store re-reconciles on a timer, so a time-based verdict fires on an idle stream and a wrong pin never wedges. | liveness | `TmuxStore.ts` | — |
| P4 | A confirmed focus op lingers (`FOCUS_CONFIRM_LINGER_MS`) so a stale snapshot cannot flap the highlight; a third pane's focus wins after `FOCUS_SUPERSEDE_GRACE_MS`; a newer focus op drops older lingering pins; a pin never names a dead pane. | ordering | `domain/store/ops.ts`, `model.ts` (`dropSupersededFocusOps`), `types.ts` | `domain/store/__tests__/ops.test.ts › holds a confirmed focus op until the linger expires, then matches`, `› holds through stale echoes of the pre-op focus`, `› holds briefly, then yields, when a THIRD pane takes focus` (examples; `dropSupersededFocusOps` is —); `tests/2-layout-navigation.test.js › Clicking an inactive tab transitions the active highlight exactly once…` [nightly] |
| P5 | Two in-flight splits never match the same real pane: ids claimed by an earlier pending op in a reconcile pass are unavailable to later ones. | safety | `ops.ts` (`reconcile`, `claimed`) | — |
| P6 | A `NewWindow` placeholder matches only a tab-typed window that already owns a pane, and the patch hides any newborn that does not match — the two conditions mirror each other exactly. | safety | `ops.ts` (`predictNewWindow`, `reconcileNewWindow`) | `ops.test.ts › stays pending until the new tab window has a pane (guards placeholder remount)`, `› patch hides a tab-typed-but-paneless newborn so it does not render as a 2nd tab` |
| P7 | On a session switch every pending op and the committed snapshot are dropped before the new session's first state, so an old op never reconciles against new ids. | ordering | `machines/actors/tmuxStoreActor.ts`, `machines/app/appMachine.ts` | `infra/store/__tests__/workflows.test.ts › TmuxStore — clear (session switch) drops committed + pending ops and notifies subscribers` (store only; the order against the first state is —) |
| P8 | A `MoveWindow` op shows the dropped tab order at once (indices renumbered in strip order) and holds it until the server's order places the window beside its anchor, so a reorder never snaps back while `move-window` travels. | ordering | `domain/store/ops.ts` (`predictMoveWindow`, `reconcileMoveWindow`), `machines/app/actions/tabOverview.ts` | `domain/store/__tests__/ops.test.ts › MoveWindow predict (tab reorder) › shows the dropped order at once, numbered in strip order, and holds it until tmux agrees`; `tests/2-layout-navigation.test.js › a tab dragged along the strip lands where it was dropped, in the client and in tmux` (the passed tabs make way mid-drag) |

## U — UI-mode records

| Id | Invariant | Kind | Stated in | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U1 | A pane has at most one copy-mode record. tmux leaving copy mode closes a record only if tmux was seen in it; a stale `in_mode` inside the exit cooldown never reopens one; a viewer never auto-enters. | ordering | `machines/app/actions/copyMode.ts`, `domain/copyMode.ts`, `docs/COPY-MODE.md` | `machines/app/actions/__tests__/reconcilePaneMode.test.ts › a snapshot reporting the previous exit does not close a record tmux has not been seen in`, `› is ignored inside the cooldown after the client exited`, `› never happens for a read-only viewer`; `machines/app/states/__tests__/copyMode.test.ts › the end of a blink closes only the view that copy left, not one opened since` (examples) |
| U2 | The optimistic resize preview holds until server geometry matches on two consecutive quiet updates; a fallback clears it after 2 s, and a stale fallback never clears a newer preview. | liveness | `machines/app/appMachine.ts`, `machines/app/actions/layout.ts` | `machines/app/states/__tests__/layout.test.ts › the 2s RESIZE_COMPLETED fallback clears a stale preview when no server update arrives`, `› …does not clobber a newer resize started within the window` (the two-quiet-updates hold is —) |
| U3 | Only the latest scrollback fetch per pane reaches the machine; a stale chunk never overwrites a fresh one, and fetches for different panes do not cancel each other. | ordering | `machines/actors/tmuxActor.ts` | `machines/actors/__tests__/tmuxActor.test.ts › interrupts the previous fetch so its result never reaches the parent`, `› different paneId fetches do NOT interrupt each other` |
| U4 | A group switch's swap is held over stale pre-confirm snapshots and dropped if either pane disappears; a parked or stash pane never receives `select-pane`. | safety | `appMachine.ts` (group switch), `ops.ts` (`predictGroupSwitch`) | `tests/7-regression-bugs.test.js › Clicking three pane-group tabs in rapid succession never flashes a non-target pane in the visible slot` [nightly] (E2E only) |
| U5 | Every menu surface — the hamburger, the native menu bar, every context menu, the session switcher, the browser's size menu — renders one spec from `domain/menus/` and dispatches each item through `executeMenuAction` or an app-machine event; no surface holds an item list of its own. | safety | `domain/menus/`, `components/menus/SpecMenu.tsx` (`useMenuDispatch`), `infra/menus/nativeMenu.ts` | `domain/menus/__tests__/specs.test.ts` (the specs per state); `components/menus/__tests__/menuActions.test.ts › sends the op of every op-backed item, the same one its keybinding hint names` |
